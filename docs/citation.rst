Citation
========

If you use face-rhythm in your research, please cite our preprint:

    Hakim et al. (2025). Spectral envelopes of facial movements predict
    intention, cortical representations, and neural prosthetic control.
    *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.10.675423

BibTeX:

.. code-block:: bibtex

   @article{hakim2025facerhythm,
     title   = {Spectral envelopes of facial movements predict intention,
                cortical representations, and neural prosthetic control},
     author  = {Hakim, Richard and Jaggi, Akshay and Heo, Gyuryang and
                Matsumoto, Hideyuki and Uchida, Naoshige and
                Watabe-Uchida, Mitsuko and Datta, Sandeep Robert and
                Musall, Simon and Sabatini, Bernardo L},
     journal = {bioRxiv},
     year    = {2025},
     doi     = {10.1101/2025.09.10.675423},
     url     = {https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.10.675423},
     note    = {Preprint}
   }

A machine-readable ``CITATION.cff`` at the root of the repository carries
the same metadata and is picked up automatically by GitHub's "Cite this
repository" button.
